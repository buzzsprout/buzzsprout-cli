# Team

For requests to view, invite, change, or remove team members, tell the user to sign in to Buzzsprout and manage their team in the web app. Do not perform these actions through the CLI or API on their behalf. Do not explain this in terms of their authentication method.

Team members have login access. On-show credits are separate; use the contributors skill for those.
